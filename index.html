About

I'm a final-year Ph.D. candidate in Biomedical Engineering at Texas A&M University, in the Computational Cardiovascular Bioengineering Laboratory. My research fuses deep learning, medical imaging, LLM systems, and computational biomechanics.

I turn complex biomedical data into actionable diagnostics — predicting infarct location from cardiac strain, inferring tissue mechanics from hemodynamics, and mapping pathology reports to standardized oncology codes. I combine physics-based simulation with modern AI: UNets, transformers, physics-informed neural networks, and ontology-aware RAG agents.

I'm seeking AI Research Scientist roles across medical AI, LLM systems, and computational biology — where rigorous science meets patient impact.

Research

Six threads where physics-based simulation and modern AI meet clinical data.

01

Multi-fidelity deep learning for myocardial infarction

A UNet combining large-scale simulated rodent-heart data with scarce human MRI labels to predict infarct location from left-ventricular strain — accurate scar detection without LGE imaging.

Advanced Science 2025PyTorch · UNet · Transfer Learning
02

Physics-informed neural networks for cardiac mechanics

A PINN embedding hyperelastic laws to predict myocardial stress–strain and infer tissue parameters — perfect three-way separation of healthy, mild, and severe pulmonary hypertension.

Under reviewPINNs · Inverse Problems · FEM
03

OncoTree code prediction via ontology-aware RAG

An end-to-end LLM/agentic pipeline mapping free-text hematopathology reports to standardized OncoTree codes — FAISS retrieval, hierarchy-aware expansion, and a constrained LLM agent with confidence, evidence, and review flags.

Clinical AI prototypeRAG · LLM Agent · Streamlit
04

LLMSurv — explainable survival modeling

An LLM-based explainable deep survival model over the UNOS lung-transplant dataset (25,000+ records, 73 features), pairing survival analysis with SHAP for interpretable risk factors.

Under reviewLLMs · Survival · SHAP
05

Spatio-temporal GNN for cardiac stiffness

Non-invasive estimation of collagen microstructure and its contribution to cardiac stiffness from imaging-derived motion, using a spatio-temporal graph neural network.

Revision submittedGNNs · Medical Imaging
06

Non-invasive pulmonary artery pressure estimation

Segmenting pulmonary vessels from high-resolution CT and training a physics-informed surrogate on CT features + FSI pressures to predict distal pressures without full simulation.

In preparationCT Segmentation · FSI · Transformer
